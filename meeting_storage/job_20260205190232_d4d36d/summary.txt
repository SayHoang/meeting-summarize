## 1. Meeting Objectives
    * The primary purpose of the meeting was to present the final prototype of a new remote control device, evaluate it against established market research criteria (fashion trends, usability, and innovation), and determine if the design is ready for production and marketing.

## 2. Discussion Topics
*   **Hardware & Technical Specifications:**
    *   **Power:** Use of a kinetic charger/battery, potentially integrated into the clamshell hinge to capture energy from opening/closing the device.
    *   **Connectivity:** Standard infrared (IR) transmission using standard chips; the sender is located at the front of the bottom layer.
    *   **Internal Design:** Dual Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs) connected via cabling through the hinge.
    *   **Interface:** A dual-layer interface featuring a black-and-white LCD touchscreen on the top section and physical rubberized buttons (Volume, Channel, Power) on the bottom.

*   **Aesthetics & Material Design:**
    *   **Form Factor:** A "clamshell" design to protect the screens and buttons.
    *   **Materials:** A plastic internal body with a "spongy," rubberized outer casing that is changeable for customization.
    *   **Style:** "Bright and funky" designs inspired by fruit and vegetable colors to align with current hip fashion trends.
    *   **Ergonomics:** Inclusion of anti-RSI (Repetitive Strain Injury) rubber buttons.

*   **Feature Set:**
    *   **Standby Function:** Included for power efficiency.
    *   **Voice Recognition:** A "find-me" feature where the user shouts a prompt and the remote responds "I'm here" to aid in location.

*   **Performance Evaluation:**
    *   The team conducted a consensus-based scoring session using a 7-point scale (1 being "True/Excellent" and 7 being "False/Poor") across six criteria (A-F).

## 3. Key Questions & Answers
*   **Question:** Can the kinetic battery be moved to the top section of the clamshell?
    *   **Status/Answer:** Answered. The team agreed that placing it in the top section might be more effective as it captures motion from flipping the device open and shut.
*   **Question:** Is the voice recognition software cost-effective?
    *   **Status/Answer:** Answered. Research indicates these systems are now easy to source and relatively inexpensive.
*   **Question:** Will the logo interfere with the exterior design?
    *   **Status/Answer:** Answered. The logo will be inset or engraved (bezelled) on the inside to avoid cluttering the external "look and feel."
*   **Question:** Does the design effectively minimize RSI?
    *   **Status/Answer:** Answered/Partial. The team acknowledged this was their weakest area (scoring 4.25), but they believe the large, spaced-out buttons are the best possible solution within current constraints.
